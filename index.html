⚠ What this doesn't prove

This shows a strong correlation between heavy discounting and negative margin — it doesn't prove discounting alone causes the loss. Deep discounts often get applied to already-low-margin categories (like Tables and Bookcases) to move slow inventory, so some of this effect is products that were already thin-margin, discounted further. Worth separating "bad discount policy" from "bad product economics" before recommending a fix.
